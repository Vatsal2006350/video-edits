
The JacHacks promo reel is two layered systems on a raw talking clip (reference: "JacHacks in Mumbai.MOV", rebuilt for Michigan as `1-yc-startups/32`).

**Hook (0 to ~5.6s) — `bin/kinetic-hook.py`, composited BEHIND the speaker.** Words arrive where they are spoken, in Playfair Black Italic gold with a tight glow, and cascade top-left → top-right → mid → bottom. The matte comes from hyperframes `remove-background` on the window (see `embed-behind.sh`); keep the bg/fg frames rather than re-matting on every text change. **Layout is decided by the silhouette, not by taste:** at torso height ~370px of letters vanish behind the body, at head height only ~190px, and the band above the head (y 278-440) loses nothing. So the hero word (JACHACKS) goes above the head where it is fully readable, and the words that are *allowed* to be half-hidden (HACKATHON, SEPTEMBER) go behind the chest and legs — that occlusion is the effect. Measure the silhouette per frame from the fg alpha before placing anything. Small secondary lines centred behind the torso are simply invisible; "THE 4TH" was dropped for that reason.

**Body — `bin/promo-caption.py`.** Anton caps, white with black stroke, keywords in RGB(255,64,0) measured off Vatsal's frames, words appearing as spoken, `skip_before` so the hook window carries no caption. Logo cards have **no white panel** — every logo in `~/Code/video-edits/logos/` is knocked to true transparency and sits on a soft shadow. Wordmarks are wide: four across can only ever be tiny, so cards lay out **two per row** in fixed boxes. Photo cards up to 660px. Card cues come from the aligned word times (Lovable 11.94s, Base44 12.56s…), not guesses.

**Hierarchy rule (Vatsal, on the first kinetic pass: "looks very weird… not all needs to be behind").** Six spoken words all rendered as gold serif behind the body reads as a word pile, and a half-hidden supporting word reads as broken, not intentional. What works is *one* face per role: hero moments (JACHACKS, $10K, 200+, MICHIGAN) in Playfair Black Italic, **maize #FFCB05 with a Michigan-blue #00274C offset shadow**; the phrases around them in small white tracked Avenir caps, in *front*; only the hook hero goes behind the matte. Every word has a `life` — it leaves after its own beat. The first version keyed leave-time off the *next word in the list*, so "IN PRIZES" hung on for 12s because the next item was 200+. Anton captions are `mute`d while a hero is up so they never double. Logo wordmarks are recoloured **white** (`*_w.png`): the black ink vanished against his shirt and the water; coloured marks (heart, sun, green eye) are left alone.

**Round three, from Vatsal's frames:** thin white tracked Avenir on a bright cloudy sky is invisible — "font and color horrible choice, barely readable". The answer was *one family with size hierarchy*, not two families: support words in white Playfair with the same blue offset, heroes in maize. A support word placed away from its phrase reads as a stray ("HACKATHON gets below weirdly") — keep phrase members adjacent (THE FOURTH / HACKATHON stacked top-right). The behind-body hero works best when only the *bottom* of the letters tuck behind the head (JACHACKS at y430 with head top ≈450, ~10% of ink covered) — fully readable and still "behind my face a little". Two collision classes to check numerically every time: kinetic words vs the **Anton caption band (y1380–1500)** the moment captions resume at 5.6s, and anything vs the **face band (y450–620)** — a hero sized 200 from y340 reaches the forehead. Hero glow bloom reaches ~14px past the glyph box, so the punch-in cap needs a 44px margin against the 724px centred limit, not 16.

**Three traps this format hit, all the same root cause:** anything centred on x=540 is bounded by twice the centre-to-rail distance — **724px** — not the safe-box width. It caught the caption wrap, the sponsor row, and the kinetic slides (a "from right" entrance, a glow bloom, and the 1.18× hero punch-in all crossed the rail; entrances are clamped to the box and the punch scale is capped so the arrival frame still fits).
