# Alternating reaction meme

Use this for a topical product/news moment where the punchline is the creator's
reaction to a reveal. It deliberately keeps the reference reel's proven grammar
without copying its creator footage.

- 1080x1920, 30 fps, 10-15 seconds.
- Alternate a human reaction with brief official/source footage on hard cuts.
- Put the first reveal on the first strong audio hit.
- Keep official horizontal footage letterboxed on black. Do not fake a vertical crop.
- One persistent sentence split into top setup and bottom consequence.
- Keep text inside x=59..918 and y=278..1536.
- Prefer creator footage; never use product-dashboard screenshots as reaction B-roll.
- Preserve a source URL and audio hash in the receipt.

Builder: `bin/alternating-reaction-reel.sh`.
