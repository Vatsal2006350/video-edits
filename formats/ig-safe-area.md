
Instagram draws its own UI over a posted reel and it eats far more of the frame than it looks: **top 14.5%** (status bar + the "Reels"/"Trial reels" header, back arrow, camera), **bottom 20%** (username, caption, audio ticker, tab bar), **right 15%** (like/comment/share rail). On 1080x1920 the usable box is **y 278-1536, x 59-918**.

Vatsal caught this after posting — his hook text landed under the "Trial reels" header and was unreadable.

**Why the obvious fix does not work:** the clean band above his head is only y 278-477 in the talking-head clips (his hair starts at 477), too shallow for a hook with a hero word. The band that is both outside IG chrome and off his face is the **chest zone, ~y 1000-1400**. Hooks and mid-reel hero lines live there now; burned captions moved to `margin_v 500` so they clear IG's own caption block.

**The right-hand rail is the constraint everyone forgets.** Centred text is centred on x=540, but the safe box (59..918) is *not* symmetric about 540, so the binding limit for a centred line is `2*(918-540) = 756px`, not the box width. Nine OR reels shipped with the gold hook text running under the like button before this was caught. Both renderers now fit against it automatically: `fisheye-text.py` re-renders at shrinking `target_w` until the **warped** bbox fits (the barrel warp magnifies the centre ~20%, so measuring the pre-warp text is useless), and `hero-type.py` defaults `max_w` to `safe_right - x`.

**How to apply:** `bin/ig-safe.py view <reel.mp4> <out.jpg> [times...]` renders the zones and mock chrome over real frames so a collision is visible — **look at it for every reel before sending**. `bin/ig-safe.py zones` prints the box and the build defaults. There is no pixel-based `check` any more: it was removed because detecting burned text from pixels is unreliable in both directions (it flagged night city lights as text, and a loosened version flagged bright walls). To assert placement automatically, threshold for the *known* text colour in the *known* band — that is what caught the nine OR reels. Separately: mid-reel text must be timed from `aligned.json`, not estimated — a 2s error there put "no one gives a shit about GPA" on screen while the captions had moved to the next sentence.
