# Reel audio identifier (no downloads — attribution read only)

For any IG reel, the song name lives in the rendered audio link. Give me a list
of reel URLs and I read each one's audio in your logged-in Chrome:

  document.querySelector('a[href*="/reels/audio/"]').textContent   // "Artist•Song"
  + the audio id from the href (…/reels/audio/<ID>/)  → the reusable-sound page

You then open that audio in the IG app and tap "Use audio" — licensed, and it
puts your reel on the trend's audio graph (burned-in MP3s do neither). This is
strictly identification; no audio is extracted or downloaded.
