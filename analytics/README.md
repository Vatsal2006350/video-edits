# Reel analytics

Account analytics are private and ignored by Git. Copy `reel-performance-template.csv` to
`reel-performance.csv`, then record each post at 24 and 72 hours.

The primary decision metrics are:

- average watch time divided by reel duration;
- shares/sends divided by reach;
- saves divided by reach;
- follows divided by reach.

Always record whether a reel was Trial or regular and whether audio was attached natively.
That prevents format, distribution mode, and audio choice from being conflated.
