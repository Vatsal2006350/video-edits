## Test protocol

- Publish each first as a Trial Reel to non-followers.
- Do not change cover/caption during the first comparison window.
- At 60–90 minutes, record 3-second hold, average watch time, shares per view,
  profile visits, and follows per view.
- Kill a variant below the account median on both hold and follows/view.
- Promote a variant above 0.45% follows/view; create two hook-only mutations while
  keeping its reveal timing and receipts unchanged.
- The simulation file is a scenario range, not a guarantee or Instagram-internal forecast.
