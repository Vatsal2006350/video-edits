# Reels research pilot — 2026-09-11

## What the local data actually supports

The strongest first-party comparison in the repository is the receipt-template pair in
`VIRAL-FORMAT.md`: the desk/webcam opener earned 3,693 likes versus 488 for the scenic
opener on roughly 490 followers, while the receipts, copy, timing, and audio were held
constant. That is a 7.6x like difference attributable primarily to the opener. Treat this
as a useful directional result, not a complete performance model: the repo has no exports
for reach, views, watch time, retention, sends, saves, posting time, or follower/non-follower
distribution.

The usable inventory is already large: 151 non-phone reel exports, 20 reusable JSON edit
specs, 83 GB of PhotoKit-derived footage, a verified footage index, 17 local music files,
and builders for receipt, OR-choice, card, glow-up, travel, talking-head, and bounce formats.
All 172 input references in the JSON specs resolve as of this audit.

## Pilot question

Does an authentic, founder-at-work opener plus a verifiable receipt outperform scenic or
polished montage openings for `vatsal.returns`, and does honest failure framing improve
sends/saves without hurting completion?

## Six-reel pilot

Run three matched pairs. Keep duration, posting window, caption length, CTA, and receipt
order fixed within each pair. Change only the named variable.

| Pair | Control | Variant | Existing material | Primary read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — opener | scenic founder hook | authentic desk/keyboard hook | `IMG_1867` or Kerala lake vs `IMG_8286` | 3-second hold, average watch time |
| B — framing | “Potential-Maxxing” win with real product metric | “Flop-Core” failure followed by the same receipt | Amplify dashboard/card assets plus desk footage | sends/reach, saves/reach |
| C — audio ownership | current in-app trend attached natively | creator-owned spoken cue over a music bed | same 3+ clips and edit | non-follower reach, audio-page uses |

Recommended order: A1, B1, C1, A2, B2, C2, one per day in the same posting window. Do not
interpret raw likes as the winner. Pair A is the highest-confidence test because it
replicates the only strong first-party result already present.

## Build concepts using existing assets

1. **A scenic:** “What building at 19 looks like” over Kerala lake, then the existing YC
   sign, product laptop, and desk receipts.
2. **A desk:** identical copy and receipts; only the first 2.7 seconds switch to
   `~/Downloads/content/IMG_8286.mov`.
3. **B potential:** “unlocking my full potential” followed by one real, readable Amplify
   metric and a working-product shot. No unverifiable revenue/customer claim.
4. **B flop:** “my recent founder flops” followed by one specific failure, then the same
   Amplify receipt. The failure must come from Vatsal; do not invent it.
5. **C trend:** the stronger A cut with a currently rising audio attached in Instagram.
6. **C owned:** the same edit with a short original spoken cue such as “show the receipt,”
   mixed with a bed so it remains template-eligible with at least three clips.

## Measurement contract

Capture at 24 hours and 72 hours for every reel:

- publish timestamp and whether it was Trial or regular;
- views/plays and reach;
- follower and non-follower reach;
- total and average watch time;
- 3-second view rate and completion rate, if exposed;
- likes, comments, shares/sends, saves, profile visits, and follows;
- audio name, audio use count at posting, and whether it was attached natively;
- exact reel file, hook family, footage opener, duration, CTA, and caption.

Normalize the decision metrics: average watch time / duration, sends / reach, saves / reach,
and follows / reach. Declare a pair winner only after 72 hours. With one post per cell this
is a pilot for choosing the next batch, not statistical proof.

## Scale decision

- If desk wins A again on normalized retention, make authentic work footage the default
  opener and reserve scenic footage for payoffs.
- If Flop-Core wins sends/saves, alternate proof and failure stories instead of shipping a
  wall of achievement reels.
- If owned audio is competitive on non-follower reach, invest in a repeatable spoken cue
  and seed variants on the same audio; otherwise keep attaching timely audio natively.
- If no cell wins clearly, do not multiply variants. First add the missing retention and
  reach export, then repeat Pair A with two new hooks.
