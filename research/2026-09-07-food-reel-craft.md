# Restaurant-reel editing standard (agent research, 2026-09-07) -- applied to the Farmyard, Adimali footage

Venue facts for on-screen tags (restaurant-guru / FavHiker): Farmyard Restaurant, Chattupara, Kochi-Madurai road, Adimali (Idukki),
Kerala 685561; Rs 200-400/person; Google 4.1 from 2,946 reviews; 7:30-22:00 daily; reviewers name meen pollichathu, fish curry
meal, thali with two fish fries. "Since 2017" is the owner's word only.

Structure: open mid-action on the payoff (leaf slap / pour / first cut), never "walk in, sit down"; first cut < 1.5s, resolve the
hook by 5s; hook 1-3s, body 70-80%, payoff in the last 10-20%, then cut dead or a 2s location line. Shots 2-5s, straight cuts,
a visual change every 1.5-3s; static shots > 4s are the drop-off zone. Ranking = watch time, likes, sends (Mosseri Jan 2025).
Voice: owner heard far more than seen (doc grammar, J/L cuts); 3-4 sound bites of 2-5s = 12-16s of owner audio in a 40s reel; keep
signature dish, method, heritage/years, price. Text: one curiosity line + sparse tags (dish name, price, location/hours at the
end); text in the centre band; fonts Montserrat Bold / Poppins / Bebas / Playfair accent. Sound: -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP; music 4-5 dB
under speech and ducked further under the owner; a half-second silence before a tactile SFX sells it; sync sizzle/ladle to frame.
Picture: fix WB first (plate whites drifting green -> magenta), gentle S-curve, warm reds/oranges on kitchen shots; slow-mo only
from 60fps+ sources; ramps on the pour/plating so the payoff lands inside 5s; whip pans hide the cut in blur; match cut round
thali -> round plate. 30fps timeline for phone footage.
The one thing: are they still watching at second 3, and does the promise at 0s pay off by 5s. So: NEVER open on the intro or
the restaurant wide. Open on the leaf.

Plan A (chosen): 0-1.4 leaf slapped down (slap SFX, bed silent) + "Kerala's fish thali / Rs 200-400 a head" -> 1.4-3 rice on the
leaf, owner VO J-cuts in ("variety of fish... thali") -> plating -> chef at the stove under "fish pollichathu, steamed in a banana
leaf" + lower-third "Meen Pollichathu" -> owner on camera for ONE line ("since 2017") + "Farmyard, Adimali" -> Vatsal eating,
first bite, music ducked -> Vatsal's intro line as the verdict beat -> overhead thali reveal (payoff) + "Fish curry meal, Rs 200-400"
-> restaurant wide + "Kochi-Madurai road, Adimali. 7:30-22:00", cut dead.
Plan B: Keith-Lee verdict cut, no music. Plan C: ASMR cinematic, one owner line (ranked last: nothing to send).
