# Footage index template

Keep the real `FOOTAGE.md` local. Record only verified source facts here; directory names
and filenames are not evidence of what a clip contains.

## Collection name

- `relative/or/absolute/path.mov` at `00:00.000` — subject, action, framing, orientation,
  audio condition, stabilization requirement, and any restrictions.

## Exclusions

- List clips or seeks that must not be used and explain why.

## Claims supported by footage

- Record the exact spoken or visible evidence for any factual on-screen claim.
